# Growth playbook — CarFable

Positioning (from NORTHSTAR): **the place your car's history lives.** Every public
vehicle page is the product AND the ad — a shareable, trust-building artifact.
Growth = getting those pages in front of (a) enthusiasts who document builds,
(b) sellers who want provenance, (c) buyers who trust documented cars.

## The wedge (use this everywhere)
"Selling a car? Attach its CarFable link — full documented history — to your
listing." Works on Craigslist/FB Marketplace/Autotrader/BaT-style audiences.
Nobody else gives a private seller a credible history page.

## Now (free, this month)
1. **Technical SEO** (orchestrator can do): robots.txt allowing public pages,
   sitemap.xml (public vehicles/posts/users), landing page copy for logged-out
   visitors (value prop + App Store badge once live), verify OG cards on all
   public pages. Public vehicle pages should rank for "<year make model> service
   history" long-tail over time.
2. **Own dogfood as demo**: owner's 4Runner public page = the showcase link used
   in every post/comment/bio.
3. **Communities (owner, authentic)**: 4Runner forums + r/4Runner (owner owns
   one — post the build/history page where allowed), r/projectcar,
   r/askcarsales / r/UsedCars threads about documenting maintenance, Tacoma
   World-style forums. Rule: be a member who shares their car, not an advertiser.
4. **App Store launch moment**: once approved — Product Hunt launch + Show HN
   ("Show HN: I built a service-history social network for cars"). Prepare
   assets: 3-4 screenshots, 1-paragraph story (built for my own 4Runner).
5. **Short-form video** (highest ceiling, cheapest): 15-30s clips — "scan a
   receipt → it becomes a service record" is genuinely demo-able. TikTok/
   Reels/Shorts, hashtags #carmaintenance #cartok #projectcar. 2-3/week.

## Next (after first users)
- **SEO content**: /blog with high-intent posts — "car maintenance log
  (app + template)", "how to document your car's history for resale",
  "what records add value when selling". These queries convert.
- **Referral loop in-product**: "Add your other car" prompt; share buttons after
  adding an event; watermark "carfable.com" on exported/shared images.
- **Partnerships**: detailers/shops offer customers a documented history;
  used-car flippers who want provenance pages.
- **Email**: launch list from hello@; monthly "your car this month" digest.

## Measure
- GA/Plausible on web (not installed yet — decide privacy-friendly option).
- App Store Connect analytics + Play Console once live.
- North-star metric: # vehicles with ≥3 history events (activated garages).

## Don't (for now)
Paid ads (no budget/LTV), influencer payouts, cold outreach. Revisit with traction.
